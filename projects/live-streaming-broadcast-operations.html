Live Streaming and Broadcast Operations

Practical experience supporting live broadcast and streaming workflows. This entry describes operational work (monitoring, recording, troubleshooting and coordination) rather than software I developed.

Hands-on operational work, not a software product I developed. It is included because it shapes how I design tools for real workflows.

The challenge

Live workflows don’t allow retakes. Feeds arrive over different protocols, need watching end to end, and have to be fixed quickly when something goes wrong.

What I did

Operational support across live events and playout: monitoring and recording live events, troubleshooting stream feeds, handling SRT-to-UDP workflows, testing and coordinating RTMP streams, and monitoring playout, including live and repeat logo changes.

Key responsibilities

  • Live-event monitoring and recording
  • Stream-feed troubleshooting
  • SRT-to-UDP workflow handling
  • RTMP testing and coordination
  • Playout monitoring and live/repeat logo changes

How it fits together

  1. 1Feeds arrive over SRT or RTMP
  2. 2SRT-to-UDP workflows are handled
  3. 3Streams are monitored and live events are recorded
  4. 4Playout is monitored, including live/repeat logo changes

What this demonstrates

An operator’s understanding of how live broadcast systems behave under real conditions: context that makes it easier to design software and automation that fit real workflows.
